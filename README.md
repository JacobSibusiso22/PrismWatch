## Problem

RPA support teams often rely on multiple tools, reports, and manual checks to monitor automation environments. This fragmented approach makes it difficult to quickly identify queue backlogs, resource issues, SLA breaches, failed processes, and recurring exceptions.

As automation estates grow, support teams require a centralized solution to provide real-time visibility into operational performance, reduce manual effort, and enable proactive issue management.

---

## Solution

PrismWatch is a centralized RPA Monitoring & Intelligence Platform designed to provide a single source of truth for automation operations.

The platform consolidates data from multiple automation and support systems, processes operational metrics, and presents actionable insights through interactive dashboards, automated alerts, health checks, and intelligent reporting.

By transforming operational data into meaningful insights, PrismWatch enables support teams to proactively identify risks, improve robot performance, reduce downtime, and enhance service delivery.
